Safety First, Recovery Always.

Professional guidelines for storing, consuming, and disposing of clinical medicines safely.

🛡️

Storage Guidelines

Store in a cool, dry place.
Keep away from direct sunlight.
Keep out of reach of children.
Refrigerated items must be 2-8°C.

Consumption Safety

Check expiry date before use.
Complete the full course.
Don't self-medicate antibiotics.
Report side-effects immediately.

Disposal Protocol

Don't flush medicines.
Peel off labels before throwing.
Mix with undesirable substances.
Return to store for safe disposal.

Critical Warning

Never split or crush sustained-release (SR) or enteric-coated tablets without clinical consultation. Doing so can cause immediate release of the full dose, leading to toxicity or gastric harm.
