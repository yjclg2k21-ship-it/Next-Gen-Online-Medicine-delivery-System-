Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload a prescription?
You can upload your RX during the checkout flow or via the 'Prescriptions' tab in your dashboard. Our clinical pharmacists will verify it within 15-30 minutes.
What are the delivery charges?
Standard Delivery is ₹25 (Free for orders above ₹1000). Emergency Delivery is ₹70 and arrives within 15-35 minutes.
Can I return medicines?
Yes, returns are accepted for unopened packaging within 24 hours of delivery. Use the 'Request Reversal' button on your order details page.

Still have questions?

Our customer success team is available 24/7 for operational assistance.
