Privacy & Data Protection

At MediMitra, we prioritize your clinical data privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect your health records and personal information.

We do not sell your clinical data to third-party advertisers. All prescription uploads are stored in an encrypted environment (AES-256) and are only visible to authorized pharmacists and clinical reviewers.

Terms of Service

By using MediMitra, you agree to provide accurate clinical information. Fraudulent prescription uploads or misuse of emergency delivery services will result in immediate account termination and potential legal action.

All medicines must be sold via verified clinical partners holding Form 20 or 21 drug licenses in India. MediMitra acts as a platform facilitating these transactions.

Refund & Cancellation

Orders can only be cancelled before they are "Accepted" by a pharmacy. Once accepted, cancellations are subject to a processing fee. Refunds for damaged clinical goods are processed within 3-5 business days.
