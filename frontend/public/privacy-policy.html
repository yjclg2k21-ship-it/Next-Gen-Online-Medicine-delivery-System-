Privacy Policy

How we protect your clinical data.

MediMitra respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your clinical and personal data. This Privacy Policy details our data handling practices.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information to provide better medical services. This includes:

  • Personal Details (Name, Phone, Email, Delivery Addresses)
  • Clinical Data (Uploaded prescriptions, ordered medicines)
  • Financial Data (Wallet balances, transaction history)
  • Telemetry (GPS data for delivery partners only)

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for order fulfillment, AI-driven generic medicine recommendations, and sending vital health alerts (like expiry or refill reminders).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Prescriptions and addresses are shared securely only with verified Vendor Pharmacies and Delivery Partners explicitly assigned to your dispatch.

4. Data Security

All clinical documents and passwords are encrypted. Our infrastructure undergoes regular security audits to prevent data breaches.
