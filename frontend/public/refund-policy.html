Returns & Refunds

Our clinical return protocols.

At MediMitra, your health and safety are our primary clinical concerns. Please read our return and refund policy carefully.

1. Return Window

We accept returns for eligible items within 7 days of the delivery date. The items must be unused, in their original clinical packaging, and with all safety seals intact.

2. Non-Returnable Items

Due to health, safety, and hygiene reasons, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Prescription Medicines (Schedule H, H1, X)
  • Temperature-controlled items (e.g., Insulin, Vaccines)
  • Opened medical devices and health monitors
  • Personal hygiene products

3. Refund Processing

Once your return is picked up by our logistics partner and inspected by the vendor, the refund will be initiated to your original payment method or your MediMitra Clinical Wallet. Bank refunds typically take 5-7 business days to reflect.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, expired, or incorrect medicine, please raise a return request within 48 hours via your user dashboard, providing a clear proof image.
