Support Categories
How long does emergency delivery take?
Our Emergency Delivery service aims to fulfill your clinical needs within 15-35 minutes, depending on your proximity to the nearest verified pharmacy partner.
Is a prescription mandatory for all medicines?
Prescriptions are mandatory only for "Schedule H" and "Schedule X" medicines as per clinical regulations. Standard wellness products and OTC medicines can be ordered directly.
How do I verify a pharmacy's authenticity?
Every pharmacy partner on MediMitra undergoes a rigorous KYC process where we verify their drug license (Form 20/21) and GSTIN. You can view their verification badge on their profile.
Can I return my medicine if it's incorrect?
Yes, we have a 24-hour hassle-free return policy if the delivered medicine is incorrect, damaged, or expired. Please use the "Return" button in your Order History.
